# Market teardown: what does the Vietnamese diet-ad category reward?

Type: research
Status: resolved
Parent: ../map.md

## Question

Tear down what is actually running in the Vietnamese weight-loss / meal-replacement /
diet-coaching market and report what the category rewards.

Look at: Meta Ad Library for Vietnamese diet, meal-replacement, giảm cân and
nutrition-coaching advertisers; competitor pages and their creative patterns; KOL and
UGC-shaped content in the space; and any credible coverage of the Vietnamese market's
buying triggers, price framing, trust signals and regulatory constraints on health claims.

Report the recurring hook mechanics, proof types, offer framing, format conventions
(image vs video, copy length, caption structure) and the register/tone that dominates —
plus what nobody is doing, since a gap is a position.

Do not choose what we should do — this ticket reports the field.

Write findings to `.scratch/ads-doctrine/research/market.md`.

Gist of the field:

- **Law binds the copy before doctrine does.** NĐ 15/2018 Đ.26–27 requires pre-clearance
  for TPBVSK / medical-nutrition / special-diet products and forbids advertising beyond
  the registered content; the disclaimer *"Thực phẩm này không phải là thuốc…"* is
  mandatory. Khoản 2 Điều 27 **bans doctor/pharmacist/health-worker imagery and names and
  patient thank-you letters** — the white-coat proof device is simply unavailable to a
  food product. NĐ 38/2021 penalties: 40–60tr for an organisation plus a **3–5 month
  suspension of the product's công bố**, which is the real deterrent.
- **The rules tightened this year.** The amended Luật Quảng cáo, in force 1 Jan 2026,
  makes endorsers personally liable, requires them to have used the product, and requires
  disclosure of sponsorship.
- **Platform policy compounds it.** Meta bans before/after imagery, negative
  self-perception and idealised bodies (reached only via secondary sources — the
  first-party page 404'd); TikTok VN mirrors it. Practitioner banned-word lists flag
  *giảm cân, béo, mập, gầy, giảm mỡ*, teaching the substitution *kiểm soát cân nặng*, and
  block guarantee/refund language. The euphemism treadmill is itself a register driver.
- **Hook mechanics in use**: pain-mirror (*chạm đúng nỗi đau*), failed-journey confession,
  number+timebox (*85kg→55kg*, *7 ngày*), occasion pressure (Tết / hè / cưới),
  objection-preempt (*cơ địa khó giảm*), shock/scarcity, hedged promise. **No named canon
  framework appears anywhere** — the category runs on folk doctrine circulated through
  template libraries.
- **Proof types**: before/after (the default device — banned, and documented as faked),
  feedback screenshots, KOL (now damaged), origin and certification documents (45% of
  buyer concern is unclear origin), ingredient-mechanism explanation. Medical authority is
  available only to clinics, not to a food brand.
- **Offer shape**: sold as a *liệu trình*, not a unit — e.g. 25tr per 10 sessions with
  centimetre guarantees; a 500k consult refunded against the package; GLP-1 pens spanning
  800k–8tr. The funnel is **ad → Messenger/Zalo inbox → consult**, not checkout: the ad's
  job is to earn an inbox, not to close.
- **Trust is the category's live wound.** Kera (Quang Linh / Hằng Du Mục — 17.5tr units in
  two months, criminal proceedings) and Ngân 98's Super Detox X3 (sibutramine and
  phenolphthalein, criminal proceedings Oct 2025) broke celebrity assertion as a device.
  Only 0.8% of supplement discussion is branded.
- **Gaps nobody is filling**: maintenance and regain (50–80% regain at 2–5 years, and
  nobody sells the after-phase); named clinical evidence presented in Vietnamese; men;
  the comorbidity buyer; candour about downsides; visible compliance.
