# The framework spine: which frameworks govern, and what defers to what

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01, 02
Parent: ../map.md

## Question

From the canon sweep and the market teardown, choose the set. Not a menu — a spine:
one diagnostic that decides which structure a given piece takes, the structures it can
choose among, and the generators that feed it.

Resolve at least: does Schwartz's awareness × sophistication grid become the spine
(the pipeline already tags `awareness_stage`, so it is the incumbent), or does something
from the market teardown displace it? Which copy skeletons are admitted, and is the
choice among them determined or free? What gets rejected outright, and why — a framework
we consciously refuse is as load-bearing as one we adopt.

**What [the canon sweep](./01-canon-sweep.md) changes about this ticket:**

- The Schwartz grid routes to a **lead type** in the primary sources, not to a copy formula.
  The awareness → AIDA/PAS/FAB-BAB/PASTOR mapping is blog folklore with a competing blog
  mapping against it. So the spine question is really: do we route to a *lead*, and if so
  does anything downstream still need a named skeleton?
- The flows are **not interchangeable** — they differ in beat count, required proof assets
  and artifact length. Any spine that treats them as swappable is choosing wrong.
- Routing may need **three axes** (format, audience temperature, offer complexity), not one.
- The canon is overwhelmingly **structures**; the scarce thing is **generators** (RMBC's
  research + mechanism, Five Objections, VOC mining, teardown, Star–Story–Solution). Since
  the driving complaint is blandness, a spine made of more structures does not address it —
  the spine probably has to be generator-first.
- "Layer on the destination, pick on the ad" is the defensible form of the layering claim.
- **No independent efficacy evidence exists for any of these frameworks**, and the field's
  only third-party-auditable evidence (Ehrenberg-Bass, Binet & Field) disputes the DR
  tradition's premises. So the spine is chosen for *what it makes the pipeline do* —
  forcing research, mechanism, proof and a compliant opening — not because it is proven to
  convert. Say that out loud in the decision.
- Exclusions the sweep recommends on evidence: **SLAP** (its letters name funnel events, not
  writing beats). Attribution to fix wherever it is repeated: six leads = Masterson & Forde
  (not Makepeace); 12-step VSL = DigitalMarketer; ACCA = Colley/DAGMAR; FABV's V = Value.

Every later ticket on this map hangs off this answer.

## Answer

**The spine is a chain, not a menu: RMBC intake → Schwartz awareness → lead type → one house
body shape, with a mandatory mechanism beat.**

**1. Research + Mechanism run first, as a generator.** RMBC's front half is adopted as the
intake that precedes any writing — what the reader actually says and believes, and *why* the
thing works or why past attempts failed. This is the answer to the blandness complaint: the
canon sweep found the field is nearly all *structures* and short on *generators*, so blandness
was never going to yield to more skeletons. RMBC's back half (Brief, Copy) is not adopted as a
stage name — the pipeline already has a brief and a writer.

**2. Awareness routes to a LEAD TYPE, not to a copy formula.** Schwartz diagnoses; the six
leads of **Masterson & Forde (*Great Leads*, 2011 — not Makepeace)** are what the diagnosis
selects: Offer / Promise / Problem–Solution / Big Secret / Proclamation / Story, on a
direct→indirect spectrum where directness falls as awareness falls. This is the only
awareness→X mapping in the canon with a primary source. The popular awareness→AIDA/PAS/FAB-BAB/
PASTOR mapping is rejected: it traces only to low-tier blogs and a competing blog mapping
contradicts it.

Two properties of the lead mapping carry into every downstream ticket: it is **overlapping,
not one-to-one** (a stage admits two or three leads — that is the room where a creative choice
lives, and it is why a spread of leads per subject is coherent), and its **word budgets do not
survive** compression to a Meta primary text. The lead *shapes* transfer; "delay the product
for 400 words" does not.

**3. A toolkit that gets varied and tested — NOT one house shape.** *(Revised by the operator
after the first pass locked in a single structure: "in ads, cannot know which approach will be
success, so better have a set of tools and vary them to find the best one instead of defining
it now without real-life testing." That reversal stands, and the rest of this answer is written
to it.)*

The spine fixes the **chain and the floor**, never the winning shape:

- **Fixed** (because law, platform policy and the Messenger close fix them, not because they
  tested well): the RMBC intake, the awareness → lead-type routing, the mandatory mechanism
  beat, the compliant opening, and a close that qualifies while pre-selling.
- **Varied and tested**: which lead type opens (the awareness→lead mapping is overlapping by
  design — two or three leads are legitimate at any stage, and that overlap is where the
  testing happens), which body structure carries it, which proof device, which register,
  which length.

So the canon's structures stay **admitted tools, not reference**: each is retained with its
declared requirements — beat count, the proof assets it demands, the artifact length it needs —
so the writer can pick one it can actually fill, and a batch can deliberately spread across
several. What the evidence forbids is treating them as *freely interchangeable*, not using more
than one. A tool whose requirements the available proof cannot meet is unavailable for that ad;
that is a fit rule, not a ranking.

Nothing here declares a winner in advance. **The doctrine's job is to keep every produced ad
compliant, grounded and genuinely different from its siblings — delivery decides which one
wins.** This is the same conclusion the sweep reached from Meta's side: swapping a CTA or a
headline is *iteration*, while changing the structure or the lead is *diversification*, and only
the latter teaches anything.

Two consequences: the variation axis is now load-bearing rather than cosmetic
([What makes N variations different](./08-variation-mechanics.md) decides it), and a doctrine
that is explicitly a hypothesis set needs a way to learn from what ships —
[How the toolkit learns from what ships](./17-testing-loop.md).

**4. The mechanism beat is mandatory in every ad.** No ad ships without it. Three reasons, all
load-bearing: it carries the pre-sell load that the Messenger close demands
([What is an ad's job](./13-what-the-ad-is-for.md)); it persuades **without asserting anything
about the reader and without promising an outcome**, which is the only way past Meta's Personal
Attributes policy and Vietnam's công-bố limits at once; and the market teardown found the
category runs entirely on folk doctrine with no mechanism anywhere — so it is a real
differentiator, not merely a compliance dodge.

**5. Exclusions, on the sweep's evidence.** **SLAP** is excluded as a copy structure (its
letters name funnel events, not writing beats, and it has no traceable originator). The
**4 U's** are not adopted as a headline generator without a gate — urgency plus
ultra-specificity systematically manufactures non-compliant headlines here; if retained at all
it is as a *filter applied after* the compliance check, and that call belongs to
[the application table](./07-copy-application-table.md). Attribution to correct wherever it
appears in skills or KB: six leads = **Masterson & Forde**, not Makepeace; the 12-step VSL is
**DigitalMarketer's** (Edwards' own has 10); ACCA is **Colley/DAGMAR (1961)**;
Star–Story–Solution is not Collier; FABV's V is **Value**, not Verification.

**6. Why this spine, stated honestly.** **No independent efficacy evidence exists for any
framework in the field** — the sweep found none, and the only third-party-auditable evidence
(Ehrenberg-Bass, Binet & Field) disputes the direct-response tradition's premises outright. So
this spine is not chosen because it is proven to convert. It is chosen for **what it forces the
pipeline to do**: do the research, name a mechanism, declare a lead, attach proof, and close by
qualifying. Any doctrine doc written from this map must say that out loud rather than implying
the canon is settled science. Whether the evidence-based tradition gets a say above the ads
channel is now sharp enough to ticket:
[Does evidence-based marketing get a say?](./16-evidence-based-counter-tradition.md).
