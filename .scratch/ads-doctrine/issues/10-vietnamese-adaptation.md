# What survives translation into Vietnamese

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01, 02
Parent: ../map.md

## Question

The canon is Anglo-American and written for a different reader; every word we persist is
Vietnamese. Decide which of its mechanics transfer intact, which need reshaping, and which
must be dropped for this market.

Cover at least: how urgency, authority and social proof read to a Vietnamese diet buyer
versus a US direct-response reader; whether long-form lead structures survive a paid-social
caption; register and address (how the reader is spoken to); the health-claim line beyond
which copy cannot go; and what the market teardown shows the category's readers already
expect that the canon has no name for.

Output is the adaptation layer the application table is written against — not a
translation glossary.

## Answer

Read live from `voice/tone`, `voice/founder-voice`, `voice/pronouns`, `voice/vietnamese-rules`,
`voice/vocabulary` (contents not restated here — read them, never a remembered version). The
finding that shapes everything below: **the adaptation layer largely already exists in the KB**,
and in two places it independently arrived at conclusions this map reached from the canon. Where
that happens, the KB wins and the doctrine cites it rather than re-deriving it.

**1. Urgency is IMPLIED — neither manufactured nor disclaimed.** *(Revised by the operator:
"urgency should be implied, no need overdo by saying *Không cần phải quyết định ngay hôm nay*."
Correct, and it sharpens the rule — that sentence in `voice/tone` illustrates the **trait**, it
is not a phrasing to reproduce.)*

Two failure modes, both refused:

- **Manufactured urgency** — deadlines, countdowns, invented scarcity, *Push*. Barred by
  `rules/banned-words` and, more decisively, by `voice/tone`'s fourth trait, which forbids
  pressure tactics outright. This also disposes of the 4 U's *Urgent* dimension as a headline
  generator: it manufactures exactly what the voice forbids.
- **Explicit anti-urgency** — spelling out that she needn't decide today. It protests too much:
  it raises the pressure only to disclaim it, spends scarce characters on a non-message, and
  reads as a sales move about not selling.

**What is left is implication.** The reason to act now is carried by the *situation the copy
already describes* — the occasion that is genuinely approaching, the drift that continues while
nothing changes, the fact that a consultant is a real person who answers — and never by a
sentence about timing. If the copy has done its work, the reader supplies the urgency herself;
that is the only form of it this brand can carry. A real dated occasion (Tết, hè, a genuine
consultation window) may still appear as **information**, stated once, never as pressure and
never as reassurance about pressure.

**2. Authority transfers only in the founder's first person.** `voice/founder-voice` makes Kiều
My the sole narrator on every public channel — there is no separate brand voice — and requires
first person (*mình* / *chúng mình*). The canon's institutional-authority posture (Ogilvy's
brand-as-expert, the lecturing educator) therefore has to be re-voiced as *what she came to
understand over twenty years*, not what the brand asserts. The doc's own Educator register makes
this explicit, contrasting an approved personal-lens sentence against a rejected textbook one.
Practical consequence: **the institutional/clinical proof device from
[12](./12-proof-problem.md) must still be spoken in her voice** — the trials exist and she
explains why they matter, rather than the brand citing itself.

**3. Social proof survives, but only as narrative.** The category's default — assertion by
celebrity, before/after, feedback screenshots — is dead ([12](./12-proof-problem.md)).
`voice/tone`'s approved form is a specific small moment reported in her voice (the client who
climbed the stairs without losing her breath, *"Không cân nào đo được điều đó"*). So
Star–Story–Solution and the Story lead transfer intact; endorsement-shaped proof does not.

**4. The KB had already found the grammatical rule from
[14](./14-opening-beat-policy.md).** `voice/tone` trait 2's approved example addresses the reader
in second person about *her situation and her labour* — the household, the children, the
in-laws' opinions — never about her body. That is precisely the permitted/forbidden line Meta's
Personal Attributes policy draws, arrived at independently. The doctrine should cite this as
existing brand practice rather than presenting it as a new constraint, which also makes it far
easier to adopt.

**5. Long-form leads do not survive the caption; they survive the surface.** The lead
taxonomy's 100–600-word budgets are direct-mail native. `ad/creative-guidelines` §6.1 already
fixes paid cold copy as hook + 2–3 benefits + close, while organic carries the long-form
confession and story. So the rule is: **the lead's *shape* transfers, its word budget does
not**, and the long structures live on organic and in the consult — consistent with "layer on
the destination, pick on the ad" ([04](./04-framework-spine.md)).

**6. Candour becomes an active device, bounded.** `voice/tone` trait 3 already refuses to pretend
the journey is easy, and the market teardown found candour to be an unfilled gap. Adopted: ads
may name the **real difficulty** — that there are hard weeks, that bodies differ, that no
journey is a straight line — which differentiates hard against the category's *"Dễ lắm!"*
register. **Not** adopted: listing what the programme cannot do, or naming who should not buy.
Candour is about the honesty of the journey, not a disclaimer of the product; the qualifying
close ([13](./13-what-the-ad-is-for.md)) invites the right reader without deterring her.

**7. The register axis has a documented vocabulary.** `voice/founder-voice`'s three registers
(Confessor / Educator / Friend) are the natural values for the **register** coverage axis from
[08](./08-variation-mechanics.md) — a batch spanning them is spanning something the brand
already defines, and `voice/vietnamese-rules` supplies the five-level tone spectrum beneath
that. The doctrine must **not** invent a parallel register vocabulary.

**What has no KB home yet**: the mechanism beat as a named, required element. Trait 1's approved
example *is* a mechanism sentence in the founder's voice, so the practice exists without the
name. Naming it belongs to [Where the doctrine lives](./03-doctrine-home.md).

**Process note.** BrandOS timed out three times on `voice/tone` before succeeding. Any doctrine
that depends on live KB reads needs a stated behaviour for that case — the current skills'
convention is to stop rather than proceed on a remembered version, and nothing here changes it.
