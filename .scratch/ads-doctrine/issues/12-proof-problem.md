# The proof problem: what proof device is left?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01
Parent: ../map.md

## Question

The canon's persuasion structures all demand a proof beat — 4 P's *Prove*, AIDCA's
*Conviction*, PASTOR's *Transformation*. The market teardown says the devices the category
actually uses are mostly unavailable to us:

- before/after imagery — banned by Meta policy, and documented as routinely faked here;
- doctors, pharmacists, health workers, patient thank-you letters — **illegal** for a food
  product under NĐ 15/2018 khoản 2 Điều 27;
- celebrity/KOL assertion — damaged by the Kera and Super Detox X3 prosecutions, and from
  1 Jan 2026 the endorser is personally liable and must have used the product;
- guarantees and refund language — blocked by platform policy;
- and no claim may exceed the product's registered công bố, with the *"không phải là
  thuốc"* disclaimer mandatory.

So decide what Cambridge's proof beat actually is, given what the brand genuinely holds
(clinical heritage, coach-supervised programme, real client journeys) and what it may
legally say. Which devices are adopted, which are refused outright, and what the writer is
obliged to attach a claim to before it may be written.

This is a doctrine decision, not a legal review — but it must not choose a device the law
or the platform forbids, so cite the constraint each time one is ruled out.

## Answer

The premise the ticket opened with was too pessimistic. The category's *default* proof devices
are indeed unavailable to us — but Cambridge holds proof almost nobody in this market can
match, and the market teardown named **"named clinical evidence in Vietnamese" as a gap nobody
fills**. Read from `brand/proof-points` and `brand/positioning` (live, not restated here):
independent third-party RCTs at named universities, a founder who is a genuine obesity-research
figure, EU 2016/1413 compliance, 26 micronutrients, 20+ years of legal circulation in Vietnam,
and a founder who has used the product since 2004.

**Four proof families are adopted. They are also the pool the coverage axis draws from
([What makes N variations different](./08-variation-mechanics.md)) — a batch must not lean on
one of them twice.**

**1. Mechanism — the mandatory beat.** Already fixed by [the spine](./04-framework-spine.md):
*why* the thing works, or why past attempts failed. It asserts nothing about the reader and
promises no outcome, which is why it survives both Meta's Personal Attributes policy and the
công-bố ceiling. It is the floor every ad stands on, not one option among four.

**2. Institutional / clinical — existence and institution, never the numbers.** Ads may say
that independent, third-party trials at named universities exist, and that this is
third-party-run evidence rather than self-cited research (the distinction `brand/positioning`
draws against MLM competitors, and the real differentiator). Ads may **not** quote kilograms,
percentages, or any disease outcome — a weight-loss figure is an outcome claim that would
exceed the registered công bố, and a disease-reversal figure is a treatment claim, forbidden
outright for a food product. Those numbers belong in the consultation, where a person can
qualify them. This is what lets us occupy the market's open gap without making a claim we
cannot make.

**3. Human proof — three forms, all without imagery of bodies.**
- **The founder's own 20+ years.** She has used the product since 2004 and maintained the
  result, so she satisfies the 2026 endorser law by construction — an endorser who genuinely
  used it. Told as **continuity**, never as transformation.
- **Consultant spotlights.** The specialist as the proof: who she is, the morning routine of
  reviewing app data and messaging first, why the team is 100% women. `brand/proof-points`
  records these as top-quartile organically, and they involve no body claim at all.
- **Client journeys, in words only.** The moment she nearly quit and what the specialist said —
  **no before/after imagery, no weight numbers**. The client must be a genuine user and, post
  1 Jan 2026, must know they are endorsing.

**4. Documentation — promoted to a first-class device, not a footnote.** The real
product-declaration and import-licence numbers, the 20+ years of lawful circulation, EU
2016/1413 — stated in the **exact wording the paperwork uses** (`brand/proof-points` is
explicit: say "đã công bố sản phẩm" / "được phép lưu hành" with the number, never a vague
"được cấp phép", and never infer a Bộ Y tế endorsement of safety or efficacy). Rationale: 45%
of buyer concern in this category is unclear origin, "visible compliance" is an unfilled gap,
and it is the one proof a competitor selling unregistered product structurally cannot copy.

**Refused outright, with the constraint that refuses them:**

- **Before/after imagery** — Meta policy, and documented as routinely faked in this market.
- **Doctors, pharmacists, health workers, patient thank-you letters** — NĐ 15/2018 khoản 2
  Điều 27. Illegal for a food product, whatever the creative justification.
- **Celebrity/KOL assertion** as a persuasion device — discredited by the Kera and Super Detox
  X3 prosecutions, and personally liable from 1 Jan 2026.
- **Guarantees and refund promises** — platform policy.
- **Any claim exceeding the registered công bố**, and any second-person assertion about the
  reader's body — see [the opening beat](./14-opening-beat-policy.md).

**The attachment rule.** No claim may be written that does not trace to a live KB proof point
or to the paperwork; the writer names which one it drew on, so a compliance reviewer can check
the trace rather than re-litigate the sentence. Where `brand/proof-points` itself flags a figure
as unciteable in paid without a source (it does for at least one), that flag governs. **Read
the docs live every run — never a remembered version**; they are revised on their own cadence,
and the numbers in them have already changed at least twice.

## Amendment — 2026-07-30, after [Reach the actual ads](./11-observe-real-ads.md)

Two corrections from observed ads:

- **Numeric outcome claims are ubiquitous and unenforced** (*"BAY NGAY 5KG"*, *"giảm được
  8-9KG"*, *"từ 72 xuống 64cm"*). Our refusal to quote outcome numbers therefore is a **chosen
  position**, not a level playing field — and it is where the advantage sits, since we hold real
  third-party numbers we decline to shout. State it as a choice.
- **Clinical citation is already in the category** — Unicity cites international patents and the
  PDR; Vitabiotics cites a Pubmed-indexed study with asterisk-qualified footnotes (a compliance
  shape worth copying structurally). So the gap is **independent third-party university trials**,
  not "clinical evidence". Tighten the doctrine's wording accordingly — the broader claim is
  refutable in one Ad Library search.
- **Add to the refused list**: character obfuscation (`G.iảm C.ân`, `má.u`, `in.sulin`) —
  the category's standard evasion of string matching, and incompatible with a brand whose
  differentiator is documentation.
