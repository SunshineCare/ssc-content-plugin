# post-plan-sophistication-inherit

## Purpose

How the market-sophistication read reaches the three Posts planning steps and what each
one does with it. The quarter authors the read once; Approaches takes it from the quarter
payload it hands the shared core and states it once in the document's first section,
Ideate judges each declared awareness stage and its angle against it, and Schedule
sequences the month with it off the channel-plan response it already holds. No Posts step
derives, infers or upgrades a stage, and none restates the saturation ladder in its prose.
Where the quarter states no read, the absence is recorded and reported, no bar is applied
and no sequencing claim is made.

## Requirements

### Requirement: The Posts channel inherits the sophistication read and never derives one

Each of the three Posts planning steps SHALL take the market-sophistication stage and
its reasoning from what has already been handed down — the Approaches step from the
quarter payload it passed to the shared core, and the Ideate and Schedule steps from
the approved Approaches document reached through the channel plan's context. No Posts
step SHALL derive, infer, adjust or upgrade a stage of its own, and none SHALL restate
the saturation ladder's labels in its skill prose.

The quarter authors the read once and the month inherits it; the Ads channel and the
head already work this way. A channel that derives its own read produces a second,
unreviewed position that no operator approved, and the ladder itself is revised on
the knowledge base's own cadence, so a remembered copy eventually contradicts it.

#### Scenario: Each step names where the read came from

- **WHEN** any of the three Posts steps applies the sophistication read
- **THEN** it names the hand-down it took the read from
- **AND** it derives no stage of its own

#### Scenario: No ladder is restated

- **WHEN** the Posts skill files are read
- **THEN** none of them restates the saturation ladder's stage labels or their
  definitions

### Requirement: Approaches states the sophistication constraint once, in the doc and in the summary

The Posts Approaches document SHALL carry the inherited sophistication constraint —
how indirect this month's openings must be — exactly **once**, in its first section.
Later sections SHALL point at that statement rather than restating it. The step's
final summary SHALL state the inherited read, or name its absence.

The document has a hard length budget that is the forcing function keeping the later
sections terse; a constraint repeated per section spends that budget on repetition.
Stating it once and pointing at it is the anti-repetition rule this document already
follows.

#### Scenario: The constraint appears once

- **WHEN** the Approaches document is composed
- **THEN** the sophistication constraint is written in the first section only
- **AND** later sections reference it rather than restating it

#### Scenario: The summary reports the inherited read

- **WHEN** the Approaches step finishes
- **THEN** its summary states the inherited stage and reasoning, or names the absence

#### Scenario: The length budget stays a real check

- **WHEN** the document grows to carry the new sections
- **THEN** the raised budget is still measured on the draft before saving
- **AND** exceeding it is treated as a failure to compress, not as a new ceiling

### Requirement: Ideate's declared awareness stage must clear the inherited bar

The awareness stage a Posts Ideate round declares SHALL be judged, together with the
angle written under it, against the sophistication read carried in the approved
Approaches document, in that read's own terms — at the round where the step settles
each surviving idea's angle. An angle that does not clear the bar SHALL be reworked
before the idea is proposed as ready, and the round SHALL state the bar it applied.
The step SHALL make no additional strategy-brief call to obtain the read.

Sophistication constrains three different decisions at three different steps; a bar
applied only once at the top leaves the step that actually declares a stage unable to
act on it. The read already travels in the plan context this round reads, so
re-fetching it would add a call and a second chance to disagree.

#### Scenario: An angle is judged against the read

- **WHEN** the round declares an awareness stage for a surviving idea and the approved
  document carries a sophistication read
- **THEN** the declared stage and its angle are judged against that read
- **AND** the round states the bar it applied

#### Scenario: An angle below the bar is reworked

- **WHEN** an angle does not clear the inherited bar
- **THEN** it is reworked before the idea is proposed as ready

#### Scenario: No second read is fetched

- **WHEN** the round needs the sophistication read
- **THEN** it takes it from the plan context it already holds
- **AND** it issues no additional strategy-brief call

### Requirement: Schedule sequences the month against the read from the response it already holds

The Posts Schedule step SHALL take the sophistication read from the **same** channel
plan response it already fetches for its gate check, SHALL use it to sequence the
month — ordering mechanism-led, more indirect posts ahead of more direct ones where
the read says the market has already heard the direct claim — and SHALL state in one
line the rule it applied. It SHALL add no knowledge-base read in order to obtain the
read.

The step already reads the channel plan for its gate; holding the context off that
same response costs nothing. Sequencing is the last decision the read constrains, and
it is invisible unless the step says which rule produced the order.

#### Scenario: The read comes off the existing fetch

- **WHEN** the Schedule step checks its gate
- **THEN** it also holds the plan context from that response
- **AND** it makes no additional call and adds no knowledge-base read to obtain the
  sophistication read

#### Scenario: The applied rule is stated

- **WHEN** the calendar is proposed and the read is stated
- **THEN** the summary states in one line the sequencing rule it applied

### Requirement: An unstated read is a reported gap with no bar and no sequencing claim

Where the quarter states no sophistication read, the Posts Approaches document SHALL
record that fact explicitly and report it in the summary; the Ideate round SHALL say
so and apply **no** bar; and the Schedule step SHALL make **no** sequencing claim and
SHALL say so. No Posts step SHALL assume, guess or default a stage in the absence of
one.

A guessed stage is worse than a stated gap, because a gap is visible to the operator
who can fill it at the quarter, while a guess silently becomes the constraint every
downstream step obeys.

#### Scenario: The Approaches document records the gap

- **WHEN** the quarter payload carries no read
- **THEN** the document records the absence in its first section
- **AND** the summary names it

#### Scenario: Ideate applies no bar

- **WHEN** the approved document records no read
- **THEN** the round says so and applies no sophistication bar
- **AND** it assumes no stage

#### Scenario: Schedule makes no sequencing claim

- **WHEN** the approved document records no read
- **THEN** the calendar is proposed with no sophistication-based ordering claim
- **AND** the summary says the read was not stated
